In te reo Māori, hūnuku means to move or shift.

Through movement Hūnuku creates the momentum to help people and teams shift their mindset to create a culture of high performance needed to win.

Hūnuku brings practical consulting, facilitation and coaching to organisations of every size. We help people connect, align, build high-performing teams and turn ambition into action — from the first kick-off to lasting improvement.

Ways to work

Practical support for clubs, boards and community groups.

We help organisations strengthen governance, participation, community connection and the outcomes they can deliver for members, communities and funders.

Club health checkWhere are you strong, and where are the gaps?

A conversation and a short written summary of your strengths, gaps and next steps.

Plan on a page workshopCan you agree where you are heading, and what to do first?

A half-day with your board or committee, ending with a clear direction and 90-day actions.

Board and committee mentoringHow can your governance work better for your members?

Practical mentoring for chairs, presidents and committee members on roles, meeting rhythm, decisions and volunteer engagement.

Community building and engagementIs your club visible, relevant and connected to the community around it?

Chris listens to how you currently attract, engage and retain people, then compares that with current practice and what successful clubs and community organisations are doing today. Together we look at membership experience, community relationships, communications, events, partnerships and profile.

The aim is to identify where awareness or participation is being lost and where practical changes can help your organisation become more visible, more connected and increasingly recognised as a heart of its community.

Chris has tested a range of practical approaches to participation and engagement and has supported the recovery of several clubs and trusts.

You get: a clear picture of where you are today, practical opportunities to lift participation and awareness, and a prioritised plan for strengthening your role in the community.

Funding, impact and community outcomesCan you show the difference your funding is meant to make?

Connect funding opportunities with the outcomes your community needs and the benefits funders want to see delivered. We look at who benefits, what success should look like, how the activity creates lasting value, and how to demonstrate participation, reach, connection and ongoing community benefit.

The goal is stronger alignment for everyone involved: communities see tangible benefit, organisations make better use of funding, and funders can see how their investment contributes to meaningful outcomes.

You get: a clearer community-outcomes story, practical measures of success, stronger alignment between your plans and potential funding, and a better foundation for funding applications, stakeholder conversations and reporting.

Funding-ready packageHow do you make the case for support?

Shape your need, outcomes and evidence into a clear proposition for funders or councils. Move from simply asking for funding to demonstrating the community outcome worth investing in.

Any of these can include a prioritisation session, a light risk and resilience check, or a conversation about digital tools and AI for your club.
