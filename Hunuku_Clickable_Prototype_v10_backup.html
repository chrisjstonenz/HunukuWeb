Experience & capability

Experience built in complex environments, from strategy to frontline delivery.

Hūnuku draws on more than two decades of work across business performance, operational change, technology, infrastructure, customer experience, transformation and coaching — alongside hands-on community governance and leadership.

  • Creating clarity and operating rhythm: helping leaders and teams turn priorities into practical plans, visible performance and sustainable ways of working.
  • Complex operations: experience leading airport technology and support across up to 50 airports, including 24/7 critical operational environments and major recovery events.
  • Customer and product outcomes: digital self-service delivery across 20+ airports, including a kiosk rebuild that contributed to a 17% increase in completed transactions.
  • Enterprise coaching: coaching leaders and teams across operational, corporate, digital, people, safety, finance and specialist functions.
  • Community leadership: governance, volunteering, sport administration, events, participation and stakeholder engagement.
  • Connecting capability: a strong network and a natural ability to bring together people, organisations and products that can help move a goal forward.
